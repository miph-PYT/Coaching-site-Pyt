What is parts-work?

I'm a parts-work coach. In simple terms, it is my job to help you resolve the conflicts and struggles inside you.

It is those internal struggles that can take up a lot of energy and focus and can make life truly miserable. Just like when you are trying to finish a task with someone you're fighting with, all the air is sucked out. You might finish the project, sure, but it will most likely not be fun.

When you are in conflict with yourself, it can feel like you are choking. When the internal fighting stops, space opens up again. Space to breathe. Or play. Or work. Your choice.

How I work

Each session moves through four stages, and each one builds on the one before. We understand a part, we listen to what it needs in the body, we stay with it fully, and only then does the space open up.

  1. 1

    Understand the part

    We look at what a part of you is doing and why. Most of the things we fight in ourselves started as a way of keeping us safe. Once we see that, we can stop pushing them away and start working with them.

  2. 2

    Listen to the body

    Feelings and tension show up in the body first, like a tight chest or a clenched jaw. We use those sensations to find out what a part needs, so you change how you feel, not just how you think.

  3. 3

    Staying with it

    Bring what is alive in you. I am skilled at staying with difficult parts, without flinching or judging, which in turn will make it easier for you to stay there too.

  4. 4

    More room to breathe

    When your parts stop competing, energy returns. You think more clearly, get curious again, and feel more like your kind, confident self.

  5. 5

    Take it into your life

    The space you get back doesn't stay in our sessions. You take it into your everyday life, and see how it changes the way you move through the world.

Coaching, not therapy

I'm a coach. I don't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. We work with where you are now and where you want to go, using tools that come from therapy, like parts-work, within a coaching frame. I work with protectors, not with exiles or deep trauma. If something needs a therapist, I'll tell you honestly and help you find the right support.

My story

I always looked at people who had a passion and knew what they wanted to do with a mix of awe and resentment. For the first 30-odd years of my life, that was not me. I felt lost. I didn't know who I was, what I wanted or what I was good at. Instead, I went along with paths that were never really mine.

Until I hit the end of that path. With a master's degree in hand, I was utterly confused and mortally afraid. What have I done? A turning point opened something up for me, and I got to revisit the darkest parts of my history and allow myself to really feel that pain, for the first time in my life. With that came the opportunity to grieve and to be there for myself. Everything changed from there.

From there I trained as a coach, and I took a deep dive into nonviolent communication and parts-work. I helped out at retreats, held space for people in difficult emotional states, and started facilitating workshops of my own. Over the years, that practice has taught me as much as any course: how to stay present with someone who is struggling, and how to meet what is difficult without rushing to fix it.

Today I help people stay with what they usually run from, until they can meet it with kindness themselves.

The parts of us we avoid often hold the key to what we actually want. I am pretty good at staying with them, without flinching or judging, which in turn will make it easier for you to stay there too.
