Parts work

The core idea in parts work is that you are not one voice. Think of when you say you want to start speaking up for yourself. Whenever you try to do it you get scared and get mad at yourself afterwards. The pattern repeats itself and nothing changes.

In parts work, we focus on getting to know the scared part of you as well as the other. Understanding their feelings and needs and working with them, instead of getting annoyed at them.

Next time, you might notice the fear again and be able to take care of that first, freeing up the energy you need to actually speak up for yourself!

That is the power of parts work.

The power of parts work

Every part of you developed for a reason. The good girl or good boy learned that being easy, helpful or quiet kept you safe and loved. Other parts, the loud one, the angry one, the wild one, got sent far away, outcasts from your internal family.

None of them are the problem. They're all trying to help, often with strategies that made sense long ago. When we listen to them with curiosity instead of judgment, they tend to soften, and there's more room for the whole of you.

What happens in a session

  1. 1

    Notice

    We start with something real: a reaction, a stuck place, a feeling in the body. That's usually a part asking for attention.

  2. 2

    Get to know it

    We turn toward it and ask: what are you trying to do for me? What are you afraid would happen if you stopped?

  3. 3

    Make room

    With understanding, parts can relax their grip. You get to choose more freely, and the hidden ones get a seat at the table.

Good to know

You stay in charge. We only go where you want to go, at your pace, and nothing needs to be dug up to be useful.

Parts work is coaching, not therapy. If you're carrying heavy trauma, I'll be honest about whether a therapist is the better fit.
