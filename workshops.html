Workshops · Copenhagen

Ever tried sitting down with your inner critic?

That's usually not our first impulse. But in parts-work, that's exactly what we do!

  • It's not good enough yet.What are you trying to protect me from?
  • Who do you think you are?What are you afraid would happen if you stopped?
  • Just one more check before you send it.Thank you. I've got this one.

In my workshops we sit down with one part of us at a time, over a cup of coffee and a notebook. We ask it what it's trying to do for us, and what it's afraid would happen if it stopped. And we notice what happens when we meet it with curiosity and openness.

The idea behind parts-work is simple: you're not one person fighting yourself. You're more like a group, and every member has a good reason for what it does.

Workshops

How the workshops work

  • Small groups, in person, sitting down. Everyday clothes, no touch.
  • In English or Danish. No experience needed, just a notebook and some curiosity.
  • Everything is an invitation. You decide how much you share; writing and listening count as taking part fully.
  • This is not therapy. It's a space for reflection and learning. We stay with everyday patterns, not old wounds.

Bring a workshop to your space or team

I run these workshops for co-working spaces, workplaces and communities, as a lunch session or an afternoon break. Write to me and we'll find a format that fits.

And if something comes up that you'd like to look at more closely, you're welcome to book a free 20-minute call.
