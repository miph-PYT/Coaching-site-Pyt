What led me here.

I work a lot on the kinky edge of what normative society deems acceptable. I've found spaces where those parts of me got to be present without needing to hide. So I feel a special affinity to people who themselves feel like they have outgrown their container.

I have taken a coaching education in 2019 at Sofia Manning and have since then worked with many coaching clients get in touch with who they truly are and helping them achieve their personal and professional goals.
